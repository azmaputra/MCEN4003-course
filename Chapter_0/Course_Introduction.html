Course Introduction

Welcome to MCEN4003 Vibration. This page explains what the unit covers, how the twelve modules fit together, and how each module page works, so you know what to expect before you start Module 1.

OverviewAbout this unit

Every machine and structure vibrates. Most of the time it is harmless; occasionally it cracks pipes, wrecks bearings, shakes buildings and shuts down plants. This unit takes you from the physics of a single vibrating mass to diagnosing faults in real machines and pipework. You will learn how engineers describe a vibration, model the structure that is vibrating, predict how it responds, measure it in the field, and decide what to do about it.

The twelve modules build on each other in three stages. Work through them in order: each module answers a question the previous one leaves open.

Stage 1 Vibration fundamentals Modules 1–5

  1. Module 1Principles of VibrationDescribing a vibration, modelling real systems, natural frequency and damping.
  2. Module 2Forced VibrationWhat happens when a machine keeps pushing: resonance, frequency response and phase.
  3. Module 3Vibration IsolationStopping vibration travelling from a machine into its foundation, and the other way round.
  4. Module 4Vibration AbsorberAdding a tuned mass to cancel vibration at a troublesome frequency.
  5. Module 5Flexural Vibrations of Beams and PlatesContinuous structures, mode shapes and many natural frequencies.

Stage 2 Measurement and maintenance Modules 6–7

  1. Module 6Understanding Signals and Vibration MeasurementSensors, sampling and the FFT: turning a measurement into a spectrum you can read.
  2. Module 7Maintenance PracticesReactive, preventive and condition-based maintenance, and where vibration monitoring fits.

Stage 3 Diagnosing machines and piping Modules 8–12

  1. Module 8Unbalance and MisalignmentThe two most common rotating-machine faults and their vibration signatures.
  2. Module 9Faults in Bearings and GearboxFinding rolling-element bearing and gear faults from their characteristic frequencies.
  3. Module 10Looseness and Motor AnalysisMechanical looseness, and mechanical and electrical faults in electric motors.
  4. Module 11Belt Drives, Pumps and CompressorDiagnosing faults in common driven machinery.
  5. Module 12Piping VibrationsFlow-induced and mechanically driven vibration in pipework, and how to control it.
